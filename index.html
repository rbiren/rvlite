      1 · Verified against NHTSA

      The VIN is decoded with NHTSA's vPIC database. If NHTSA does not identify the manufacturer as Airstream, the lookup stops — nothing else is accepted.

      2 · Checked in the US

      Recall records are pulled from NHTSA's recall database by make, model and model year, with a sweep across known Airstream model names when needed.

      3 · Checked in Canada

      The Canadian equivalent — Transport Canada's Vehicle Recall Database — is queried for the same make and year, and both countries' results are shown in one common format.
